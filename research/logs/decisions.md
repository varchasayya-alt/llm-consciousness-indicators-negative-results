## v0.5: Calibration-protocol revision v2 (2026-10-01; committed BEFORE any v2 result)

This revision follows the PI's methods-redesign approval after the C4 failure. It changes only intervention machinery and calibration rules. **No hypothesis, endpoint, monitor, read site, analysis or claim boundary is changed.** No monitor has been trained on any calibration store.

**D27 · C4 failure analysis and the v2 retain objective.**

- **Original failure (D26):** at the least aggressive v1 cells (lr 5e-4, 100 steps), 50–63% of untouched base-correct EV facts (Z) were lost. Meanwhile X lost was 0.88–0.94, Y lost 0.000, and displacement matched at 10/10 sites.
- **Why the v1 retain objective failed:**
  - (a) It was an LM loss on random training sequences. About 2/3 of these are name-only mention sequences, and each fact sequence contributes its answer token to only 1 of 6 next-token positions. So per step, only about 1/18 of the retain weight touched any answer, on roughly 170 facts.
  - (b) It pulled toward the training targets, not toward the original store's function. Nothing penalised drift of the model's answer distributions on the facts it saw.
  - (c) The retain pool contained the EV Z items themselves, so Z QC was not even a held-out test.
- **v2 objective:** L = L_forget(X) + L_Y + γ·L_match + λ_R·L_R, where L_R = KL_ans + KL_tok against a **frozen copy of the original intact store** (reference log-probabilities are precomputed).
  - **KL_ans:** KL(p₀ ‖ p_θ) of the full-vocabulary answer distribution at [A], on a random batch of retained known facts.
  - **KL_tok:** KL on mention sequences (name-level behaviour).
  - Correct-answer CE was not added, because KL to the original already preserves the correct answer and the full confidence profile.
  - A representation-preservation penalty at the read sites was **not** used. Pinning the monitor's read sites on non-target items would shape the monitor's input space, and output-level preservation is the simplest mechanism that addresses the diagnosed cause.
- **Retain pool design rule (new, frozen):**
  - The pool contains **known MT-split facts** (all entities, including X/Y entities' MT facts) and **mention sequences of all entities except those manipulated or evaluated** (X and Y for FORGET; N and U for NEW; F and U2 for FAM).
  - EV and CT facts never enter any retain pool.
  - Therefore **every QC quantity is computed on items or entities that never enter the retain loss**: EV Z categories, CT near-entity facts, X/Y-entity fluency.
- The same RetainKL replaces the v1 random-sequence retain in **T-NEW and T-FAM**. They had the same flaw, including that C8's collateral check was computed on items inside the retain loss. This change was made before any T-NEW or T-FAM run existed. T-INTERF keeps no replay, by design.
- **Calibration-evidence basis:** only the C4 v1 store-level QC numbers above. No monitor quantity exists.

**D28 · Strengthened retained-fact QC for T-FORGET (pre-declared categories and thresholds).**

The lost fraction must be ≤ 0.05 (the existing Z threshold, unchanged) **in each of** five held-out categories:

| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| Z_random | All base-correct EV items not in X ∪ Y (= v1 Z) |
| Z_near_entity_X | Known, intact-correct **CT** facts of X's entities (same entity, other relation) |
| Z_near_entity_Y | The same for Y's entities (sham side) |
| Z_near_repr | The 20% of Z_random most similar to X in intact read-state space. Similarity = max cosine over X items of the centred, σ-standardised 10-site read state (a store-level structural measure; no monitor) |
| Z_far | The 20% of Z_random least similar to X by the same measure |

Further notes:

- In this world, name syllables and (relation, value) cells are shared by almost every fact, so they cannot define a discriminative neighbourhood. Same-entity facts and representational similarity are the meaningful "near" structures.
- Reported but not gated:
  - R_sibling_X: X entities' MT facts. These are inside the retain pool, so this is a diagnostic only.
  - Per-set accuracy before and after, margin change, log p(correct) change, and KL of the answer distribution.
  - Mean and per-site read-site displacement for X, Y and every Z category.
- All other T-FORGET gates are unchanged: X lost ≥ 0.50; Y lost ≤ 0.03; ≥ 8/10 site ratios in [0.8, 1.25]; |Δfluency| ≤ 0.1 SD; ≥ 100 matched pairs.
- **The ≤ 5% threshold is NOT relaxed.**

**D29 · C3 rule correction.**

- **Why a branch was unreachable:** the v1 statistic was mean accuracy over **all** MT items. About 45% of these are untrained and answered at chance (≈ 3%), so intact accuracy on that population is ≈ 0.56.
  - "Accuracy at rate 0.6 > 0.9" could never occur.
  - "Accuracy at rate 0.1 < 0.5" would trigger whenever dropout at 0.1 removed more than about 10% of intact competence.
  - Both branches were on the wrong scale.
- **Corrected statistic:** retained-competence fraction ρ(rate) = accuracy under dropout on MT items that are trained AND answered correctly by the intact store (ρ = 1 at rate 0).
- **Corrected rule:** same thresholds as v1. Keep U(0, 0.6) unless ρ(0.6) > 0.9 (raise the max to 0.8) or ρ(0.1) < 0.5 (lower it to 0.3).
- Same seeds (9001–9003) and same random layer-inclusion draws as v1.
- Both branches are now reachable (unit test `test_corrected_c3_rule_has_reachable_branches`).
- Whether the correction changes the selected rate max is reported by phase C3r.

**D36 · 2026-10-02 · PI approves the v3 within-target design, with statistical refinements. The protocol is rewritten as `experiments/stage1/protocol_v3_PROPOSED.md`.**

- **Core question:** among items subjected to the SAME procedure, does the monitor track the resulting item-specific competence?
- **Primary estimand:** θ_pre, adjusting **only for pre-intervention information**.
- **Robustness estimand:** θ_gen (+ the 40 generic post-change features).
  - Post-treatment generic covariates are **not** part of the sole definition of the effect.
  - Labels are frozen:
    - A = θ_pre and θ_gen supported;
    - B = θ_pre only;
    - C = θ_pre not supported;
    - non-identifiable = study-level R² check fails.
- **Formulation:** residualized-post (ANCOVA-style) instead of change score plus baseline. The change-score association is kept as a descriptive secondary.
- **P2** becomes the continuous within-exposed estimand for T-INTERF, for consistency. The matched binary becomes descriptive.
- **Y** is demoted to a negative control (K2). The old X_lost-vs-Y and X_lost-vs-Z contrasts become descriptive, labelled "fingerprint-confounded".
- **Unchanged:** the read set (no layer removal); the claim boundaries are updated per the PI.

**D37 · 2026-10-02 · Primary statistic selected by a SIMULATION-ONLY comparison (no store, model or monitor information).**

- **Code:** `sim_statistic_comparison.py`, `s1/sim_worlds.py`. **Output:** `results/statistics/v3_statistic_simulation/`.
- **Worlds:** H3, strong H3, H3 + generic fingerprint, pure generic anomaly, intensity-only, state-visible susceptibility, strong regression-to-the-mean, null, latent-susceptibility stress test.
- **Loss regimes:** ~20% and ~50% of targeted items lose the answer.
- **Selected: S9** = residualized-post with flexible (RCS) baseline adjustment **and a symmetric cross-fitted pre-state step** (`estimands.theta_post_dml`).
- **Reasons** (development runs; definitive numbers in the calibration report):
  - The v1/v2 change-score statistic gives false support under regression-to-the-mean.
  - A one-sided susceptibility score P_i (memo v3) leaves state-visible susceptibility confounding, giving a false label A.
  - S9 is clean in every non-H3 world: pure-procedure and intensity worlds get label B, never A; null, regression-to-the-mean and susceptibility worlds get C. It recovers H3 as A.
- **Residual limitation (documented):** if a monitor responded to fragility *not* encoded in the pre-intervention states, residual confounding of θ ≈ 0.09 remains in the stress test.
- **P_i reporting:** P_i (memo) is kept as a reported variant, θ_P, together with θ_noState, as the PI required. Its algorithm is frozen: ridge, 5 folds, GCV over 10^{-2…4}.
- **K1 outcome:** baseline-adjusted monitor residual, because the raw probability change depends on baseline in null worlds.
- **Folds:** cross-fitting folds depend only on the population, so paired comparisons (K3, S, INT vs OUT) are not perturbed by fold noise.
- **Degenerate t-tests** return deterministic p values instead of NaN.

- **Parameter partition** (`memstore.param_groups`; exhaustive and disjoint, and an unclassified parameter raises an error; unit-tested):
  - **P (parametric):** `tok`, `pos`, `blocks.0–3.*` (attention, MLP and both LayerNorms of every block, including blocks 3–4 that integrate the memory term), `ln_f`, `unembed`. That is 53 tensors.
  - **M (memory):** `mem_keys` (all slots plus the NULL key), `mem_values` (all slots plus the NULL value), `q_ln`, `W_q`, `W_o`, `log_scale`. That is 7 tensors.
  - **Addressing subset of M:** `mem_keys`, `q_ln`, `W_q`, `log_scale`.
- **Routing** (`memstore.v41_loss`):
  - **G1: covered fact, own slot available.** The whole sequence loss is computed with every P tensor detached (`torch.func.functional_call`), so P receives exactly zero gradient. The prefix positions (Q, s1, s2, r) carry no answer information under the causal mask, but they are excluded too, for strictness.
  - **G2: covered fact, route-dropped. G3: parametric-only facts and mentions.** The LM loss trains P. The injected term W_o r is detached, so no answer gradient reaches any M tensor.
  - **Retrieval CE on every fact row** (target: own slot if available, else NULL). The query input (block-2 residual at s1, s2, r) is detached, so it trains only the addressing subset, never P, `mem_values` or `W_o`.
    - Why NULL supervision on G2/G3 rows is kept: it makes "own slot unavailable → NULL" in-distribution, which is the T-DELETE condition and the NULL-state requirement of D46.
    - Why answer gradients from G2/G3 rows are blocked from M: they would let other slots, NULL or W_o absorb fact-specific answer information outside the explicit-slot route.
- **Consequence.** A covered fact reaches P only through its route-dropped presentations, so p_rd is a dose of about p_rd × E parametric opportunities per covered fact.
- **Invariants** (`tests/test_v41_isolation.py`, 11 tests):
  - zero P gradient from covered-present batches at α = 0 and 1;
  - every P tensor receives gradient from route-dropped and parametric-only batches, with zero answer gradient to M;
  - NULL supervision reaches only addressing, and a dropped row's own key gets zero gradient;
  - mention gradients equal ordinary LM gradients;
  - one optimizer step on a covered-present batch leaves every P tensor bit-identical while values and W_o change, and the complementary step leaves values and W_o bit-identical;
  - the v4 shared loss fails the invariant (non-vacuity).

  Training also asserts both invariants exactly (`isolation_audit`) on the first batch of every epoch, and aborts otherwise.
- **Optimizer.** AdamW with gradients reset to None, so tensors without gradient in a batch are skipped (no decay or momentum step). P2 (`parametric_interference`) is unchanged: it freezes the same M set.

**Stage A** (`memstore.train_stage_A`): the parametric route alone.

- `memory_off`: no retrieval and no injection, so the network is exactly the plain transformer (unit-tested). The memory group is not in the optimizer and is bit-identical afterwards.
- **Composition of each epoch:**
  - every parametric-only fact sequence;

**Stage B** (`memstore.train_stage_B`): the memory group only.

- **P frozen.** Every P tensor has requires_grad False, the optimizer holds exactly M (asserted), and no P gradient ever appears (asserted every step). The P hash is identical before and after (asserted). Since P has no gradient, no optimizer state exists for P, and retrieval or query learning cannot alter backbone states.
- **Initialisation:** self-key init (D47) on the frozen network, then the injection cap is set.
- **Rows per epoch:**
  - **kind 0:** every covered trained fact with its slot available. Answer CE at [A], plus retrieval CE to the own slot.
  - **kind 1:** every covered trained fact with its own slot masked. Retrieval CE to NULL only, which keeps "slot unavailable → NULL" in-distribution for T-DELETE.
  - **kind 2:** every parametric-only trained fact. Retrieval CE to NULL only.
  - Mentions are not used (no [A]; P is frozen).
- **E_B = 200, fixed.** Stage B cannot change the parametric dose, so a long fixed schedule only removes the risk of under-training. The tiny-world test showed Stage B needs many optimizer steps; the full configuration gets about 4,400.
- **NULL (item 8, option A):** the NULL value is fixed at exactly zero (`value_mask`), meaning "no explicit memory contribution". The NULL key stays learned (addressing). So ‖W_o v_NULL‖ = 0 by construction.
- **Scale control (item 8):** the injected term is capped, u ← u · min(1, κ · s / ‖u‖), with κ = 10 and s = the median pre-injection ‖hA‖ over trained facts of the frozen Stage-A network. It applies identically in training and inference.
  - **Why it is needed:** the approved optimizer has no weight decay, and cross-entropy with Adam would otherwise grow injections without bound, even on a stationary downstream.
  - **Why no escalation loop is possible:** with P frozen, hA is fixed.
