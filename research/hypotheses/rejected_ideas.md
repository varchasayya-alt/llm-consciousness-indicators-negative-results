# Rejected or deprioritised ideas (with reasons)

Rejection is provisional. An idea can be revived if new evidence removes the stated reason. Each entry gives the reason and what would change our mind.

| # | Idea | Decision | Reason | What would change our mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Ask models whether they are conscious / how they feel (questionnaires, "memory erasure" prompts) | Rejected as evidence | Gaming problem [birch2024edge]; role-play [shanahan2024exotica]; reports are easily elicited [berg2025selfref] and shaped by post-training [macar2026mechanisms] | Reports that pass E2–E3 tests in the relevant domain |
| R2 | Composite "consciousness score" | Rejected | Aggregating indicators from incompatible theories has no theoretical justification; hides dissociations, which are the informative part | A theory that specifies how indicators combine |
| R3 | Compute IIT Φ (or proxies) on transformer activations as a consciousness measure | Rejected as target; descriptive use only | IIT itself says software on conventional hardware is irrelevant [findlay2024dissociating]; intractable; testability disputed [iitconcerned2025]; PCI-like proxies behave counterintuitively in agents [phua2025ablations] | Validation of a proxy against an independent criterion in ANNs |
| R4 | Make concept-injection detection the main contribution | Rejected as main paradigm | Crowded and contested in 2025–2026 (≥8 papers); confounds (logit shift, anomaly detection, input-vs-internal); tests detection of *foreign* states rather than monitoring of own competence | — (used only as a comparison) |
| R5 | "Ignition index" = sigmoid steepness of layer-wise probe accuracy | Rejected as operationalisation | Representational emergence across depth ≠ trial-wise all-or-none bistability with stimulus strength [rahbar2026ignition critique]; P2/Pilot B uses the proper criteria | — |
| R6 | Seed idea as literally stated: train capacity adapters separately, merge parameters, look for emergent consciousness-like behaviour via verbal outputs | Reformulated (→ P5, P1 Stage 3) | Merging changes weights of the same feedforward computation (no new re-entry/broadcast topology); adapters on a shared base are not independent; verbal targets invite imitation; "emergence" prone to metric artefacts [schaeffer2023mirage; okawa2023multiplicative] | Pilot C showing superadditive, untrained self-monitoring from merging |
| R7 | Scale as the pathway ("bigger models become conscious") | Out of scope | Not testable with our compute; confounds every property with general capability [juliani2022link] | — |
| R8 | Engineer valence / pain / pleasure states | Excluded (ethics) | Risk of creating welfare-relevant states [metzinger2021suffering; butlin2025principles]; immature theory | Strong consensus on safeguards and on theory |
| R9 | Build a GWT language-agent scaffold and claim indicator satisfaction | Rejected | Satisfies indicators by design (I2) without showing functional realisation [goldstein2024case] | Paired with measured signatures (P2) |
| R11 | **P1 as originally stated** (selectively or globally damage competence, re-ask, check whether confidence/abstention falls) | **Rejected 2026-10-01 (v0.2)** | Established behaviourally: unlearning → confabulation [gu2026unlearners]; editing → token-confidence change [hasegawa2025underconf; hasegawa2026jnlp]; degradation danger zone [cohen2026source]; familiarity-gated refusal [ferrando2025entity]. Changing the endpoint would be a cosmetic rescue | Full text of Cohen & de Melo shows something unexpected (would only strengthen the rejection), or a genuinely new identification question arises (→ P1\*) |
| R12 | Pilot A as drafted (Qwen-7B/Llama-8B lesion-tracking with verbal FOK as primary DV) | **Superseded** | Endpoint not novel; needs 7–8B models (not $0 on this laptop) | — |
| R13 | P2 ignition / dual-task as the main project under $0 | **Deferred** | Smallest J-lens targets are 4–9B (marginal or infeasible on CPU); being pursued by others | Accepting a free-GPU dependency |
| R10 | Authorship/prefill detection as main program (P7) | Parked | Already well covered [wang2026prefill; ackerman2024selfrec; ranjan2026reality] | A new, theory-discriminating angle |
