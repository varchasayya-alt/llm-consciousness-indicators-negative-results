# Claim boundaries (Stage 1, v3; label status revised by D54 for v4.1). Written before any monitor or confirmatory data exist.

**Universal boundaries:**

- Every Stage-1 claim is **Level 1**: a computational property of *this* synthetic system.
- Level-2 remarks are allowed only in the conditional form "this satisfies an operational criterion that theory T would treat as relevant to indicator I".
- **Level-3 (phenomenal) claims are never made.**
- No result is generalised to LLMs, brains, other architectures, other world types, or other capacities.
- The larger consciousness relevance is **theoretical only**. Functional self-monitoring is one candidate component in a broader developmental / integration research programme.

## Strongest permitted claims (PI, 2026-10-02; label status revised by D54, PI 2026-10-03, before any monitor or confirmatory data)

**Only label A counts as affirmative support for the competence-monitoring hypothesis.** The statistic and the label definitions are unchanged; only their inferential status changed. The reason is the simulation-only finding D50: pre-intervention susceptibility that is spread diffusely over many state dimensions, and that the finite pre-state sample cannot recover, produced a false label B in about 25% of simulated studies and never a false label A.

| Result | Status | Strongest permitted wording |
|---|---|---|
| P1 label **A** | Confirmatory support, subject to all other preregistered gates and robustness requirements | "A separately developed monitor tracks intervention-induced changes in the **current competence** of the neural system it observes, beyond what can be inferred from unchanged inputs and measured pre-intervention susceptibility." (wording unchanged from 2026-10-02) |
| … **and** P2 supported with label **A** | Adds (unchanged) | "The tracking cannot be readily reduced to the measured generic signatures of the intervention, and it generalises across qualitatively different competence changes (targeted deletion and later-learning interference)." |
| P1 label **B** | **Not affirmative support** | "Among identically treated items the monitor's estimates were associated with competence change, but this association cannot be distinguished from residual pre-intervention susceptibility or generic state-change information. It is not evidence for competence monitoring." It may be reported as scientifically interesting, never as a positive primary result |
| P1 label **C** | No evidence | "No evidence for the monitoring hypothesis in this system." |

## Per-outcome boundaries

| # | Result | What it WOULD establish (Level 1) | What it would NOT establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **P1 label A** | Among identically targeted items, the monitor's post-intervention estimate tracks the store's resulting item-specific competence beyond pre-intervention information and beyond the measured generic change structure | That the monitor "knows", "is aware", or has higher-order *thoughts*. Privileged (non-output) access: under uniform forgetting the output distribution also carries the competence change (OUT baseline) |
| 2 | **P1 label B** | Nothing affirmative (D54): an association with competence change that cannot be separated from residual susceptibility or generic state-change information | Competence monitoring; competence monitoring as distinct from anomaly detection |
| 3 | **P1 label C** | No evidence for the proposed monitoring effect in this system | That such monitoring is impossible |
| 4 | **P2 label A** (with P1 A) | Generalisation to a second, mechanistically different, never-seen competence change with naturally arising errors | Generalisation to all competence changes or to non-memory competences |
| 5 | **θ_noState ≫ θ_pre** | Part of the unadjusted association reflects pre-existing susceptibility | — |
| 6 | **Non-identifiable** (median R²_gen ≥ 0.90, R²_pre ≥ 0.90, or R²_joint ≥ 0.95) | That in this system the competence change is (almost) determined by generic change or by pre-intervention information, so the dissociation cannot be tested | Anything about monitoring |
| 7 | **K1 ✓** | Binary support: matched lost vs retained items within the same procedure are distinguished | Causal effect free of selection on unmeasured susceptibility |
| 8 | **K2 equivalent** | A matched-magnitude, competence-preserving change does not move the monitor more than unrelated items | That the monitor is insensitive to direction-specific procedure fingerprints (Y does not match direction) |
| 9 | **F3 > 0.5 and F2 ≈ 0** | The estimate rises with newly acquired competence and is unmoved by familiarity-only exposure | That familiarity plays no role in natural settings or in LLM metacognition |
| 10 | **K3 ✓** (TP > S) | Developmental exposure to the system's own competence variation improves within-target tracking in this system | That development is *necessary*, or SOMA-type claims about brains |
| 11 | **Synergistic T×P** (consistent on the z(θ), raw θ and matched-binary d′ scales, with TP > max(T, P)) | A synergistic developmental interaction under the pre-specified contrast | "Emergence" in any stronger sense |
| 12 | **K4 ✓** | Compositional zero-shot transfer: information-seeking tracks the held-out competence change although neither module was trained on it | Intentional or agentic information-seeking (the controller is monotone in the monitor's output: *transmission*, not new computation) |
| 13 | **S gap > 0** | System-specific coupling beyond linear alignment | "Identity", "selfhood", "self-recognition" |
| 14 | **OUT ≥ INT on P1 and P2** | Competence changes are fully available from the first-order output readout in this system | That internal monitoring is never useful |
| 15 | **Study-level or methods failure** (§ failure criteria) | Only that the design failed | Any hypothesis |
